Check the field limits in the submission form before pasting. A Product Hunt account is free.

**Name:** VoiceFlow

**Tagline:** Free, open-source voice typing for macOS and Windows

**Description:**

Hold a key, speak, let go: cleaned-up text appears in any app. VoiceFlow removes filler words and applies your
spoken corrections. It's MIT licensed and uses your own free Groq key, with no accounts or analytics. Audio is
sent to Groq.

**Topics:** Mac, Windows, Open Source, Productivity

**Maker's first comment:**

[A few sentences in your own words about why you built it.]

Two things to know before you install. VoiceFlow sends your audio to Groq with your own key, so it isn't
on-device. And the builds aren't notarized or signed by a paid certificate, so macOS asks you to click Open
Anyway and Windows shows SmartScreen. I'd like to hear where it breaks.
